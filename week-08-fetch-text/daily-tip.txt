👗 OUTFITLY DAILY STYLING ADVICE (Autumn / Spring Edition)

Tip #1: The Rule of Thirds in Fashion
Avoid dividing your silhouette equally in half (50/50). Instead, tuck your top or layer with a cropped jacket to achieve a 1:2 visual ratio (short torso, elongated legs). This creates an effortless, flattering aesthetic for both casual and formal wear.

Tip #2: The Sandwich Rule
Coordinate the color of your footwear with your top or headwear (e.g., white shoes + white tee with dark trousers in between). This frames your look symmetrically and makes even simple college outfits look intentionally curated.

Tip #3: Statement vs. Foundation
Pick only ONE focal statement piece per outfit (such as a patterned overshirt, bold jacket, or vibrant shoes). Keep all remaining pieces in neutral tones (navy, beige, olive, grey, or black) to let the hero piece shine without visual clutter.
