🌿 OUTFITLY WARDROBE & FABRIC CARE GUIDE

1. Denim Maintenance:
   - Wash denim jeans inside-out with cold water only every 4 to 6 wears to prevent dye fade.
   - Air dry rather than tumble dry to preserve fabric elasticity and tailored fit.

2. Cotton Shirts & T-Shirts:
   - Avoid excessive high-heat ironing; steam while lightly damp to eliminate creases easily.
   - Store structured shirts on rounded wooden hangers to protect shoulder structure.

3. Wool & Knitwear:
   - Never hang heavy sweaters on wire hangers (this stretches shoulder seams).
   - Fold sweaters neatly in drawers or cloth storage bins with cedar balls.

4. Footwear Longevity:
   - Rotate shoes every 24 hours so inner cushioning and insoles breathe and dry.
   - Use cedar shoe trees for leather shoes and soft microfiber wipes for sneakers.
